Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

Summary: Dopamine Detox does NOT collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All your data stays on your device.

Introduction

Dopamine Detox ("the Extension") is a browser extension designed to help users reduce distracting website usage by requiring typing challenges before accessing blocked sites. This privacy policy explains how we handle user data.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. The Extension does not:

  • Collect personally identifiable information
  • Track your browsing history
  • Send any data to external servers
  • Use analytics or tracking services
  • Share data with third parties

Data Storage

The Extension stores the following data locally on your device using Chrome's built-in storage API:

  • Settings: Your list of blocked websites, custom blocked sites, and lock duration preferences
  • Statistics: Number of blocked attempts, challenges completed, current streak, and estimated time saved
  • Lock State: When your settings were locked and emergency pass usage

This data is stored using chrome.storage.sync, which means:

  • Data is stored locally on your device
  • If you're signed into Chrome, data may sync across your browsers (this is a Chrome feature, not controlled by us)
  • Data never leaves Google's infrastructure and is never accessible to the Extension developers

Permissions

The Extension requires certain permissions to function:

  • Storage: To save your settings and statistics locally
  • Tabs: To redirect blocked sites to the challenge page
  • WebNavigation: To detect when you navigate to a blocked site
  • Alarms: To manage timers and periodic cleanup tasks
  • Host Permissions: To detect and block specific social media domains

Data Retention

All data remains on your device until you:

  • Clear the Extension's data through the settings page
  • Uninstall the Extension
  • Clear your browser data

Children's Privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device and we have no access to it, you have complete control over your data. You can:

  • View your data in the Extension's settings page
  • Export your data using the built-in export feature
  • Delete all data using the reset feature or by uninstalling the Extension
